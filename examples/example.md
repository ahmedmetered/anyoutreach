## Product (the only claims allowed)

- What it is: a design service that refreshes pharmacy signage to match how people actually search for pharmacies on maps.
- The 3 pains it removes: outdated storefront that reads "closed" in street photos; inconsistent naming between the sign, Google listing and app pins; reprints done ad-hoc every few years with no plan.
- Proof points that EXIST: 14 pharmacies refreshed in Torino province since 2024; each project photographed before and after (gallery on the site).
- Pricing: refresh from 1.900 EUR per location, multi-location plans on request. Never in email 1.
- Offer/CTA of the sequence: a free 10-minute "map check" of their listing, sent as a screenshot.
- Booking method: suggest 3 times.
- Objection answers:
  - "too expensive" -> a refreshed sign photographs well in the exact map tile patients browse; one walk-in per week pays for it over the year.
  - "we just redid it" -> great; the check also verifies the map pin and photos match the new sign. Free either way.
  - "no time" -> the check needs 10 minutes and happens by email.
- Things explicitly NOT claimable: any effect on Google ranking (we do not claim SEO outcomes); municipal grant coverage.

## ICP and channels

- Who receives: the owner-pharmacist (farmacia.example addresses).
- Excluded from email: pharmacy chains with central marketing (in-person only).
